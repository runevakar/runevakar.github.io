THE CHRONICLES OF ALDRYN

THE LAST LIGHT OF NORÐR

A novel by Timo Finsterbusch

Beyond the known sea begins a journey no one planned. A story of loss, hope, ancient secrets and the question of how far a person must go when the familiar world collapses behind them.

THE STORY BEGINS

Some journeys begin with a destination. Others begin in the moment when there is no way back.

Einar knows the sea, its storms and its moods. But when a threat rises over Norðr that is greater than anything he has ever faced, a familiar life becomes a journey into the unknown.

Together with those who remain at his side, his path leads away from the known coast, across dangerous paths and dark waters, toward an island more often spoken of in legend than seen by human eyes: Aldryn.

But not everyone seeks Aldryn for the same reason. And some secrets were not forgotten. They were hidden.
