THE BAND

FIVE VOICES · ONE JOURNEY · ONE WORLD

Runevakar is more than a musical project. Five musicians combine powerful voices, Nordic soundscapes and cinematic storytelling into one shared journey through the world of Aldryn.

LIV SKOVGAARD

Lead Vocals

Liv's voice shapes the emotional core of Runevakar. From quiet, fragile moments to powerful peaks, she carries the stories of Aldryn through hope, loss and departure.

ERIK HALDOR

Lead Male Vocals

Erik's distinctive voice gives Runevakar depth and weight. His parts stand for determination, memory and the darker chapters in which every journey comes at a price.

ASTRID EIR

Female Vocals

Astrid adds warmth, openness and atmosphere to Runevakar's sound. Her vocals blend with Liv and Erik to form the layered voices of Aldryn.

SØREN FALK

Drums

Søren forms the rhythmic foundation of the band. From restrained passages to driving peaks, his playing gives Runevakar's journeys motion and dynamics.

LEIF STORM

Bass

Leif's bass connects rhythm and atmosphere. His playing gives the songs depth and carries the dark, earthy foundation on which Runevakar's Nordic world is built.
